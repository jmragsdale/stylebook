How do I use a style in Midjourney?

Open a style, set the prompt builder to Midjourney, type your subject and tap Copy. Tap the arrow button to open Midjourney and paste.

How do I use a style in ChatGPT?

Choose ChatGPT and New image, type a subject, then tap the arrow button. ChatGPT opens with the prompt filled in.

How do I restyle my own photo?

Choose Restyle attached image and a strength (Subtle, Balanced or Bold). Open ChatGPT with the arrow button, attach your photo there and send.

What does Full Access include?

Monochrome and Surreal are free. A one-time purchase unlocks every other collection with all of its codes and prompts.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in Stylebook and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Do I need other accounts?

Yes. Midjourney requires its own subscription, and ChatGPT image generation requires a ChatGPT account. Stylebook is not affiliated with Midjourney or OpenAI.
