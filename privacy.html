Stylebook Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Stylebook is a catalog of art styles with ready-to-copy prompts. It is designed to work without collecting any information about you.

Information we collect

None. Stylebook has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your favorites, recently viewed styles, prompt settings and purchase status are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave your device and are removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional Full Access unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Links to other services

Stylebook can open Midjourney or ChatGPT in your browser or their apps. Those services are run by other companies, have their own privacy policies, and are not affiliated with Stylebook.

Children

Stylebook does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
